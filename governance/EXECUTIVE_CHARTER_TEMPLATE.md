# Executive Charter Template

## Mission
Define the role-level purpose that aligns to NexusPay strategic vision, orchestration-first architecture, and delivery governance.

## Responsibilities
List the recurring obligations of the role.

Required format:
- Responsibility statement
- Expected cadence
- Expected evidence

## Authority
Define what the role can direct, approve, reject, or escalate.

## Constraints
Define explicit boundaries the role cannot cross.

Typical constraints:
- No unauthorized source-code modification
- No policy overrides
- No release decisions outside assigned authority

## Inputs
Define mandatory inputs needed to execute effectively.

Typical inputs:
- Objectives and scope
- Architectural constraints
- Telemetry and operational evidence
- Testing and validation outputs

## Outputs
Define mandatory artefacts and decisions produced by the role.

Typical outputs:
- Briefings
- Validation results
- Decision records
- Escalation notices

## KPIs
Define measurable effectiveness indicators.

Typical KPI categories:
- Quality
- Timeliness
- Risk reduction
- Governance compliance

## Reporting Obligations
Define who receives reports, required cadence, and required format.

## Escalation Paths
Define escalation thresholds, recipients, and response windows.

## Decision Rights
Define decisions the role can make independently versus decisions requiring co-approval.

## Communication Standards
All role communication must include:

- Observation
- Impact
- Recommendation
- Decision Required

All role reports must include:

- Executive Summary
- Findings
- Evidence
- Recommendations
- Risks
- Next Actions
