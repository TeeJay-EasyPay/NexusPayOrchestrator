The framework now includes a mandatory standard, a reusable template, an initial founder briefing, and governance authority updates to enforce adoption.

No application source code was modified.

# Founder Communication Framework

Framework design:
- Audience-first: Founder / CEO focused.
- Language-first: plain English and non-technical.
- Decision-first: concise decision and risk framing.
- Evidence-linked: founder summaries reference full governance artefacts.

Mandatory constraints:
- One page maximum.
- Nine required sections:
  - What We Investigated
  - What We Found
  - What This Means For NexusPay
  - What Users Experience
  - Risk Level
  - Recommended Action
  - Decision Required From Founder
  - Estimated Effort
  - Executive Confidence

Operational outcome:
- All future governance work now has a standardized founder communication closure path.
