# Founder Communication Standard

## Purpose

This standard ensures every governance activity ends with a founder-level briefing that is clear, concise, and decision-ready.

Audience:
- Founder / CEO

Primary objective:
- Translate governance findings into non-technical executive communication that supports timely founder decisions.

## Applicability

This standard applies to:
- Governance pilots
- Incident investigations
- Validation and certification sprints
- Root-cause governance activities
- Executive governance review cycles

This standard is mandatory for all governance activities coordinated under [governance/CHIEF_ORCHESTRATOR_CHARTER.md](governance/CHIEF_ORCHESTRATOR_CHARTER.md).

## Language and Style Requirements

- Use plain English.
- Avoid technical jargon where possible.
- When technical terms are unavoidable, define them in one short sentence.
- Focus on business impact, user impact, risk, and required decision.
- Keep writing concise and direct.

## Length Requirement

- Maximum length: one page.

## Mandatory Founder Briefing Sections

Every founder briefing must include exactly these sections:

1. What We Investigated
2. What We Found
3. What This Means For NexusPay
4. What Users Experience
5. Risk Level
6. Recommended Action
7. Decision Required From Founder
8. Estimated Effort
9. Executive Confidence

## Required Content Quality

Each briefing must:
- State the scope in one clear sentence.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- State risk level as Low, Medium, or High.
- Include one clear recommended action.
- Include one clear founder decision request.
- Include confidence as High, Medium, or Low with one-line rationale.

## Governance Alignment Rules

- Governance authority remains defined by [governance/GOVERNANCE_INDEX.md](governance/GOVERNANCE_INDEX.md).
- Founder briefing does not replace detailed technical governance artefacts.
- Founder briefing must reference detailed reports used as evidence.
- If evidence is incomplete, briefing must explicitly state uncertainty.

## Ownership and Responsibility

- Owner: Chief Orchestrator
- Contributors: CTO, Testing Director, and relevant executive role owners
- Approval for inclusion in governance closure pack: Chief Orchestrator

## Publication and Storage

- Founder briefing files are stored in [founder-briefings](founder-briefings).
- Naming convention:
  - FOUNDER_BRIEFING_XXX_SHORT_TOPIC.md
- The latest briefing for each governance activity must be linked from the associated governance closure report.

## Compliance Checklist

Before closing a governance activity, confirm:

1. Founder briefing created in [founder-briefings](founder-briefings).
2. All nine mandatory sections present.
3. Briefing is non-technical and one page or less.
4. Risk level and executive confidence clearly stated.
5. Decision required from founder explicitly written.
6. Evidence documents referenced.
