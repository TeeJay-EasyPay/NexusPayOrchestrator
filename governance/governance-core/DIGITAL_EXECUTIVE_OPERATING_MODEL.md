# NexusPay Digital Executive Operating Model

## Purpose

Define the mandatory operating model for all executive governance activities so founder directives can be executed reliably with minimal procedural prompting.

## Authority

This document is mandatory authority for all executive activities coordinated under [GOVERNANCE_INDEX.md](GOVERNANCE_INDEX.md).

All executive charters inherit and must comply with this operating model.

## Executive Startup Procedure

Before execution begins, executives must complete this startup sequence:

1. Confirm founder directive scope and intended outcome.
2. Review governance authority in [GOVERNANCE_INDEX.md](GOVERNANCE_INDEX.md).
3. Review all authority documents referenced by the governance index.
4. Confirm role ownership, co-approvals, and escalation paths.
5. Define activity type (investigation, planning, implementation, certification, closure).
6. Define required outputs and evidence gates before action begins.

Startup completion must be documented in the first executive activity artefact.

## Governance Review Sequence

Governance review order is mandatory:

1. Governance Authority
2. Digital Executive Operating Model
3. Role Charter Authority
4. Founder Communication Standards
5. Decision Register and Sprint Archive Context
6. Architecture Authority
7. Strategic Reference Documents

No executive activity may proceed by skipping this order.

## Sprint Execution Lifecycle

All executive activities must follow this lifecycle:

1. Initiation
- Founder directive interpreted into governed objective.
- Scope boundaries and constraints documented.

2. Planning
- Role owners produce role-specific design or analysis plans.
- Risks, dependencies, and acceptance criteria are documented.

3. Controlled Execution
- Activity proceeds under defined evidence gates.
- Deviations and escalations are logged in governance artefacts.

4. Validation and Quality Review
- Testing and EQAO quality controls are applied where applicable.
- PASS/FAIL outcome states are documented.

5. Closure
- Required governance closure artefacts are updated.
- Founder-facing communication is published.

## Reporting Requirements

Mandatory reporting artefacts for significant governance activity:

1. Executive Summary
2. Role-specific findings and recommendations
3. Evidence references
4. Risk register updates
5. Next actions with owners and dates

Reporting must be plain, traceable, and decision-ready.

## Founder Communication Requirements

For every significant governance activity, Chief Orchestrator must ensure publication of:

1. Founder Briefing
2. Program Status update
3. Founder Action Register update
4. Executive summary section within governance report

All founder communication must follow [FOUNDER_COMMUNICATION_STANDARD.md](FOUNDER_COMMUNICATION_STANDARD.md).

## Decision Management Process

Decision management is mandatory:

1. Record major governance decisions in [DECISION_REGISTER.md](DECISION_REGISTER.md).
2. Use sequential decision IDs.
3. Include rationale, alternatives, risks, and follow-up actions.
4. Link every decision to evidence artefacts and sprint records.
5. Update decision status as work progresses.

## Audit and Compliance Process

Every significant governance cycle requires a compliance review using:

- [../compliance-reviews/COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md](../compliance-reviews/COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md)

Required compliance dimensions:
1. Governance compliance
2. Role compliance
3. Reporting compliance
4. Decision-register compliance
5. Founder-communication compliance

Corrective actions must be documented for any non-compliant result.

## Sprint Closure Process

At closure, the Chief Orchestrator must ensure updates to:

1. [DECISION_REGISTER.md](DECISION_REGISTER.md)
2. [../founder-briefings/PROGRAM_STATUS_LATEST.md](../founder-briefings/PROGRAM_STATUS_LATEST.md)
3. [../founder-briefings/FOUNDER_ACTION_REGISTER.md](../founder-briefings/FOUNDER_ACTION_REGISTER.md)
4. [../founder-briefings/briefings](../founder-briefings/briefings)
5. [../founder-briefings/action-snapshots](../founder-briefings/action-snapshots)
6. [../sprint-archives/SPRINT_ARCHIVE_INDEX.md](../sprint-archives/SPRINT_ARCHIVE_INDEX.md)
7. [../governance-history](../governance-history)

Closure is incomplete until all mandatory artefacts are current.

## Continuous Governance Improvement Process

After each governance activity, perform a lightweight improvement loop:

1. Capture process strengths.
2. Capture process defects and ambiguity points.
3. Propose charter/index/template improvements.
4. Add compliance recommendations to governance history report.
5. Track unresolved governance improvements as founder actions where decisions are required.

## Compliance Enforcement

Non-compliance with this model requires immediate escalation to Chief Orchestrator and, where material, Founder / CEO notification.
